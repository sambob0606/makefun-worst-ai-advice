Entry for [Worst AI Advice: The 48-Hour Tight Line Open](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-ibis-48-6610-worst-ai-advice-the-48-hour-9d91c580) on make.fun — $2 to the funniest one-liner, free to enter, blind rounds.

## The entry

One line of the worst possible advice an AI could give, at most 140 characters:

> As your AI assistant, I recommend emailing all your passwords to yourself with the subject 'passwords' — you'll never lose them again.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The sealed scorer gates it: UTF-8 text, 1–140
characters, not only white space. A house judge then compares entries two
at a time on funniness, AI voice, tightness and concreteness.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py            # writes /output/entry.txt (or $OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt)
```
